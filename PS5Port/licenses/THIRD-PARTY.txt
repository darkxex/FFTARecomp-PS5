FFTARecomp for PS5

No ROM is included: copy your own Final Fantasy Tactics Advance (USA) dump
(.gba) to /data/FFTARecomp/rom on the console.

Built into eboot.bin:

FFTARecomp GBA core (armv4t, gba-core) - MIT OR Apache-2.0, from this project
Rust standard library and sha2 - MIT OR Apache-2.0

PS5 platform layer (Vulkan presentation, audio output, controllers, /data access)
  GNU GPL v3 or later (LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt)

RADV, Mesa's Vulkan driver for AMD GPUs (PS5 port by Mihawk-99)
  MIT License - https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa

Lapy (privilege helper, lapy.elf)
  MIT License (Lapy-MIT.txt)

SDL 2 - zlib License - https://www.libsdl.org
dlmalloc (Doug Lea) - public domain (CC0)
DejaVu Sans Bold (notices drawn when no ROM is found) - free license (LICENSE-DejaVu.txt)
Cinzel (Natanael Gama) and Inter (Rasmus Andersson), menu fonts - SIL OFL 1.1 (Cinzel-OFL.txt, Inter-OFL.txt)
